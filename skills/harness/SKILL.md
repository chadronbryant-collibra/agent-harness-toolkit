# Help the person do the next useful thing

Read START-HERE.md in the downloaded toolkit folder for setup. Use its docs/README.md to find the relevant guide. The toolkit folder may be separate from the current workspace. Ask for its location if needed; do not assume installing this skill copied the guides. Load the few pages the task needs, rather than the whole repository.

## For a task

Keep the goal, permission, sources, allowed files, completion checks, budget and stopping condition clear. Continue approved steps without repeated prompting. Use another agent only when authorized, give it separate ownership, and check what it returns.

## For an improvement

Select at most three observations from the named task. For each, explain the behavior to preserve, what was expected, what happened, the source of that conclusion, the proposed fix and the next check.

Label the cause as unknown, inferred or verified. Reproduce a failure with a case that would expose it, apply approved fixes, then check the result. Compare a later outcome before claiming time or cost savings. Unknown measurements stay unknown.

Ask before changing budgets, permissions, retention or routing policy. Do not automatically save the conversation as memory.

## For stalled work

Identify whether it is a missing prerequisite, unavailable capability, denied permission, software defect or uncertain action. An unchanged denial ends that route. Inspect the exact task, process and possible effects before retrying.

Finish when the agreed checks pass, or explain the exact dependency that needs the person. Keep independent approved work moving while that dependency is unresolved.
