# Keep your setup useful

**Review the parts you use, preserve what works, and make one change at a time.** You do not need to run a full technical audit every time you open Claude.

[← All guides](README.md) · [Check your setup](acceptance.md)

## Do a light check-in

After an update, repeated problems, or about a month of regular use, ask:

> Review the parts of my setup I actually use. Check for outdated guidance, unnecessary cost, broken connections and anything we keep doing twice. Recommend the smallest useful improvements.

Look at whether your usual tasks finish well, your models still fit, spending is understandable, and your references and saved preferences are still relevant. A change to what Claude may access, spend or retain needs your agreement.

## Update without losing your choices

1. **Keep the current version.** Make a private backup of the settings and files you want to preserve.
2. **Unpack the new version beside it.** Read what changed before replacing anything.
3. **Ask Claude to compare.** Review the relevant instructions and settings, keeping your edits.
4. **Try one workspace.** Open a fresh session and repeat a familiar task.
5. **Keep a way back.** Retain the previous version until you are happy with the result.

> Help me update this toolkit. Show me what changed, preserve my preferences and work, and check a familiar task before we switch fully.

The first-time installer refuses to overwrite an existing setup. It is not the update process. Optional reminders may point to the old toolkit folder; Claude should review those paths before moving or removing it.

## When work stops unexpectedly

> Inspect the last progress note, the current files and any running task. Tell me what definitely finished, what might still be running, and what needs checking before another attempt.

A quiet window or a missing result does not prove a task stopped. A previous attempt may have changed files or incurred cost. Claude should check those possibilities before starting again.

If the toolkit reports a busy workspace, an unfinished run or changed settings, do not delete the record just to continue. Ask Claude to inspect it. [The recovery reference](reference/commands.md#inspect-an-interrupted-run) names the relevant files for that investigation.

## Undo a change

Ask Claude to compare the current setup with the saved version and restore only the agreed parts. If you edited settings after enabling reminders, those edits must be preserved during removal. The toolkit will refuse an automatic restore when the settings no longer match what it wrote.

## Remove the toolkit

> Help me remove this setup. Explain what will be removed and what will remain. Preserve my work and edited files. Ask separately about saved memory and backups.

Remove optional reminders first. The installer can remove unchanged files it originally created; it keeps files you have edited, task records, spending records and memory. Removing the folder or disabling a feature does not erase information held in backups or external services.

## Turn a problem into an improvement

Choose one problem worth fixing. Ask Claude to explain what happened, show the source of that conclusion, try a focused fix and check whether the same problem still occurs. Review a later task before claiming the change saves time or money.

**A good result:** your usual work still succeeds, your preferences remain intact, and you know how to recover or remove the change.
